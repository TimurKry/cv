# SimpleStudy — Senior Full-Stack Engineer

## Why did you decide to apply for this position?

Three reasons, in order of weight.

First, the product. I was studying computer science until my program was
discontinued by the university, so the problem of staying motivated once the
external structure disappears is not abstract to me. A consumer AI product that
keeps 1.5M students going, adding 40-50k a week, is something I can reason about
as a user and not only as an engineer.

Second, the stack is what I already work in every day: TypeScript, React,
Next.js, Node.js and Tailwind. My most recent project is a mobile-first PWA on
Next.js 16, React 19, Tailwind v4 and Supabase, with Playwright and Vitest
suites, built end to end in an AI-assisted workflow with Cursor and Claude
(github.com/TimurKry/dvizh-patrol). Given that SimpleStudy is itself an AI
product built by a small team, that way of working feels native rather than
novel.

Third, the scope. I have spent six years either owning projects alone - seven
freelance clients from requirements through to launch - or being the person a
business unit came to directly, as at Straumann Group, where I modernized an
internal B2B ordering tool end to end. A small team where one person owns a
surface completely is the setup where I do my best work.

One thing I would rather say myself than have you find out later: my production
experience is web rather than React Native. It is the same React and TypeScript
foundation and I am confident about the transfer, but I have not shipped a React
Native app.

## What are your salary expectations?

EUR 65,000 - 75,000 gross per year for full-time employment.

If the engagement is as an independent contractor, the equivalent range is
roughly EUR 85,000 - 95,000 per year, since based in Germany that means carrying
health insurance, pension contributions and unpaid leave myself.

Both are negotiable, and I am open to weighting cash against equity differently -
at an early-stage company I would rather have a fair mix than the highest
possible cash number.
